Delivery checklist

  1. Verify the purchase order.
  2. Confirm the delivery address.
  3. Schedule the packing team.
  4. Count each item.
  5. Check the packaging.
  6. Record the dispatch date.
  7. Attach the dispatch note.
  8. Arrange the pickup.
  9. Record the pickup time.
  10. Confirm receipt.

One source table and one source list should remain distinct logical elements.
